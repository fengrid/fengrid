Guide

Generating palettes

Five swatches, one palette. Pick a harmony rule — pleasing mix, analogous, complementary, triadic, monochrome — then press Space (or Generate) to roll new colors. Locked swatches (the padlock on hover) keep their color while the rest reroll. Drag a swatch sideways to reorder; ✕ removes it.

Editing a color

Click a swatch to open the editor: HSL sliders plus a hex field. Each swatch also shows its contrast ratio against white and black with WCAG badges — useful for picking text-safe colors.

Saving & the sidebar

Save palette stores it in a local SQLite database with a name and tags. The sidebar lists saved palettes with search, sorting, and a tag cloud; click one to reopen it.

Exports

  • CSS vars — custom properties named after the palette.
  • JSON — the raw color list.
  • SVG board / PNG board — a palette board image with hex codes and auto-generated color names, ready for READMEs and style guides (PNG is a 2× render).

Keyboard

  • Space — reroll unlocked swatches
  • Esc — close dialogs

Palettes saved here can be applied to Projects as the color scheme of a whole asset pack.
